## 🐗 1. Phase I: The Durotar Foundation (Levels 1-12)

### ⛳ Objectives
Get your 5-man group synchronized, secure your primary flight paths, and gather your starting baseline gear.

* **Levels 1-6 (The Valley of Trials):** 
  * Spawn in and group up immediately. 
  * Burn through all starting area quests as a unit. Split tags cleanly on high-congestion targets like Sarkoth.
* **Levels 6-10 (Sen'jin Village & Razor Hill):** 
  * Move north to Sen'jin Village, accept all quests, and move out to the Echo Isles early to get the swimming sections out of the way. 
  * Move up to Razor Hill. Complete the standard quest hubs including Thunder Ridge and the Zalazane chain.
* **Levels 10-12 (Orgrimmar Arrival):**
  * Head north to the capital city.
  * **📍 FLIGHT PATH UNLOCK:** Grab the **Orgrimmar Flight Path** up on the central tower.
  * Train your level 10 class abilities and weapon skills.

## 🏜️ 2. Phase II: Entering The Barrens & Pre-Bag Setup (Levels 12-15)

### ⛳ Objectives
Establish your main Kalimdor travel infrastructure and prepare your quest log for the massive level 15 instance cascade.

* **The Road to the Crossroads (Levels 12-14):**
  * Walk west from Durotar across the Southfury River into The Barrens.
  * **📍 FLIGHT PATH UNLOCK:** Grab **The Crossroads Flight Path** immediately.
  * Bind all Hearthstones to **The Crossroads**.
  * Tackle high-density kill quests nearby (Plainstriders, Zebra Hooves, Prowlers).
* **Expanding South & The Spark (Level 14):**
  * At Level 14, run your entire group south past the field of giants down to Camp Taurajo.
  * **📍 FLIGHT PATH UNLOCK:** Grab the **Camp Taurajo Flight Path**.
  * **🧳 SLEEPING BAG STEP 1:** Walk just south of Camp Taurajo to coordinates `(46.1, 74.3)`. Interact with the **Burned-Out Remains** to complete *"...and that note you found"* and pick up the next leg: *Stepping Stones*.
  * Push the group over the level 15 threshold via remaining Barrens quests.

## ⛓️ 3. Phase III: The First Dungeon Sequence (Levels 15-20)

### ⛳ Objectives
Execute a highly accelerated dungeon route. This phase transitions you through three distinct dungeons, utilizing quest turn-ins to explode your leveling velocity.

* **Dungeon 1: Ragefire Chasm (RFC) — Level 15**
  * Group Level Requirement: 15
  * Run the instance once to full completion. Turn all quests in at Orgrimmar.
* **Dungeon 2: Wailing Caverns (WC) — Levels 16-18**
  * Execute a full 9-boss clear of the caverns. Defeat Mutanus the Devourer.
  * Turn in your massive quest payload back at the Crossroads. You will emerge around level 18.5.
* **Dungeon 3: Ruins of Lordaeron (RoL) — Levels 18-20**
  * Head to the Orgrimmar Zeppelin tower and fly straight to Tirisfal Glades.
  * **📍 FLIGHT PATH UNLOCK:** Grab the **Undercity Flight Path** in the courtyard.
  * Enter the *WoW Forever* **Ruins of Lordaeron** portal directly inside the courtyard ruins. Clear the dungeon entirely to push your group cleanly over level 20.

## 🏰 4. Phase IV: Shadowfang Keep & The Sleeping Bag Sprint (Levels 20-22)

### ⛳ Objectives
Clear SFK, establish your northern Eastern Kingdoms network, and use advanced Hearthstone routing to secure your Cozy Sleeping Bag.

* **Dungeon 4: Shadowfang Keep (SFK) — Level 20-21**
  * Run south into Silverpine Forest.
  * **📍 FLIGHT PATH UNLOCK:** Grab **The Sepulcher Flight Path**.
  * Kill Archmage Arugal and turn in all objectives at The Sepulcher.
* **Setting up the Eastern Kingdoms Base:**
  * Run south from Silverpine into Hillsbrad Foothills.
  * **📍 FLIGHT PATH UNLOCK:** Grab the **Tarren Mill Flight Path**.
  * **🔥 IMPORTANT STEP:** Have your entire group bind their **Hearthstones to Tarren Mill**.
* **The Intercontinental Sleeping Bag Run:**
  * **🧳 SLEEPING BAG STEP 3 & 4 (Stonetalon Mountains):** Fly back to Orgrimmar, run west into Stonetalon Mountains. Grab the **Sun Rock Retreat FP**. Head to the trail northeast of town at `(50.9, 52.3)` leading up to the abandoned campsite at `(40.6, 52.4)`. Loot the **Pocket Litter** to claim your free **12-slot Old Toolbox**. Walk 50 yards north over the hill to `(39.6, 49.8)`, click the **Mound of Dirt** and accept *Wet Job*.
  * **🧳 SLEEPING BAG STEP 4 (Loch Modan):** Take the Zeppelin back to Undercity. Run south into Hillsbrad, head east into Arathi Highlands. Grab the **Hammerfall FP**. Head south through the Wetlands, sneak through the Dun Algaz tunnels directly into Alliance-controlled Loch Modan. Navigate to the northern Stonewrought Dam at `(49.4, 12.9)`. Drop down the ledge safely to loot the **Eagle Figurine**.
  * **🧳 SLEEPING BAG STEP 5 (The Claim):** With the figurine looted, have your entire 5-man group **use their Hearthstones simultaneously to return to Tarren Mill**. Run directly northeast to Thoradin's Wall `(87.3, 49.6)`. Complete the wagon-jumping mechanic to scale the wall, go through the interior room, and look right to find the hanging **Messenger Bag**. Click it, then loot the **Hastily Rolled-Up Satchel** right below it. 
  * **🎉 REWARD UNLOCKED:** You now possess the **Cozy Sleeping Bag** and 5x **Student Fodder**!

## 🌊 5. Phase V: Reaping the Rewards & Blackfathom Deeps (Levels 22-24)

### ⛳ Objectives
Clean up the out-of-order final sleeping bag step and use your new 3% stacking group experience buff to completely dominate Blackfathom Deeps.

* **Cleaning up Step 2 (Westfall):**
  * Run south to Stranglethorn Vale from Hillsbrad or take the Grom'gol Zeppelin.
  * **📍 FLIGHT PATH UNLOCK:** Ensure you have the **Grom'gol Base Camp Flight Path**.
  * Swim safely up the western coast of Stranglethorn until you hit the shores of Westfall. Walk inland to Alexston Farmstead at `(37.1, 50.1)` and click the cart wreckage to turn in your final outstanding map piece.
* **Marching to Ashenvale:**
  * Fly from Grom'gol back up to Orgrimmar. Exit Orgrimmar west to enter Ashenvale.
  * Travel west along the main road past Splintertree Post until you reach the northwestern shores.
  * **📍 FLIGHT PATH UNLOCK:** Grab the **Zoram'gar Outpost Flight Path**.
* **Dungeon 5: Blackfathom Deeps (BFD) — Levels 22-24**
  * **THE BUFFER ROUTINE:** Right outside the BFD cavern entrance on the beach, have **one player** deploy their Cozy Sleeping Bag. Have all 5 members sit inside the bag together for **3 consecutive minutes**.
  * **⚔️ THE PAYOFF:** Your entire party now carries a **3-stack (+3%) active experience buff** lasting for 2 hours. Storm down into the instance portal, clear all 7 bosses, and maximize your elite-kill XP returns!

## 🚀 Phase VI: The Post-BFD Dungeon Cleave & Quest Meta (Levels 24-40)

### ⛳ Objectives
Shift into a "Melee/Spell Cleave" routine. Completely bypass open-world congestion by cycling through highly condensed dungeon networks, stopping only to clear massive quest hubs in single sweeps. Always drop the Cozy Sleeping Bag on entry.

* **Levels 24-29: The Thousand Needles & Razorfen Run**
  * **The Zone Payload:** Fly to Camp Taurajo and run south into Thousand Needles. Grab the **Freewind Post FP**. Sweep up every single quest in the Shimmering Flats (The Raceway) in a single loop—these have zero travel time and massive item drop clusters.
  * **The Dungeon Focus: Razorfen Kraul (RFK):** Head to the Southern Barrens border. Cleave RFK until the group hits level 29-30.
* **Levels 29-36: The Scarlet Monastery (SM) Domain**
  * **The Base Setup:** Fly back to **Tarren Mill** and ride north into Tirisfal Glades.
  * **The Dungeon Focus: Scarlet Monastery (Graveyard -> Library):** 
    * *Levels 29-32:* Clear SM Graveyard. It has low mob health and massive density.
    * *Levels 32-36:* Shift into SM Library.
* **Levels 36-40: The Cathedral Vaults & Mounts**
  * **The Dungeon Focus: Scarlet Monastery (Armory -> Cathedral):** 
    * Continuously farm Armory and Cathedral. Your 5-man group can chain-pull these corridors with your Cozy Sleeping Bag buff active. You will exit SM at level 40 with a massive gold stack ready for your **Level 40 Mounts**.

### ⛳ Objectives
